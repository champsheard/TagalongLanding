The right amount of sharing

A trip link, not an open door.

Sharing is optional and limited to an active trip. There’s no public profile and no ongoing location feed after the trip ends.

01

02

The link is for following a trip in progress, not for finding you later or tracking you between walks.

Good to know

A few things worth knowing.

Clear answers before you head out.

Is “Safest” a guarantee?

No. “Safest” is an estimate based on available lighting data. That data can be incomplete or out of date, and it cannot tell you what conditions are like right now. Use your judgment and choose the route that feels right to you.

Does Tagalong guide me turn by turn?

No. Tagalong helps you plan and compare walking routes; it is not turn-by-turn navigation. Check your route before you set off and use your own judgment along the way.

Who can see a shared trip?

Anyone holding the private trip link can view that shared trip. Send it only to someone you trust. Sharing is limited to an active trip and the link expires automatically.

Will Tagalong call for help if something happens?

No. Tagalong does not contact emergency services, and it is not an emergency service. If you need emergency help, call 911.

Do I need to make an account?

No account is needed to plan a walk or share an active trip link. Optional saved contacts are a separate feature.
